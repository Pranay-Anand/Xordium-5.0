A student-built signal for the next generation.

Xordium 5.0 is a student-led college technology and culture event designed for people who want to make, compete, learn, and connect. It brings different kinds of thinkers into the same electric space — from coders and gamers to artists, explorers, and first-time builders.

Mission

Make technology more human.

Create approachable, high-energy spaces for students to test ideas, share skills, and find collaborators.

Vision

A campus culture that builds.

Champion curiosity across technology and culture, and give every participant a place to contribute.
